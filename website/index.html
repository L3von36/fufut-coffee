Our Story

From Ceremony
to Community

Fu Fut Coffee began with a simple belief: that the warmth of an Ethiopian coffee ceremony belongs everywhere. We pair heritage brewing methods with a modern café experience.

Every bean is ethically sourced from Yirgacheffe, Sidamo, and Guji regions. Every dish honors traditional flavors while welcoming contemporary palates.

Heritage

The Coffee Ceremony

A centuries-old tradition of roasting, grinding, and brewing — shared with warmth and intention.

1

Roasting

Green beans are roasted over an open flame, filling the room with rich, aromatic smoke.

2

Grinding

The roasted beans are ground by hand using a traditional mortar and pestle.

3

Brewing

Coffee is brewed in a jebena — a clay pot — and served in three rounds: Abol, Tona, and Baraka.
