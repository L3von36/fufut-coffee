Our Story

Where Ethiopia's Coffee Soul Comes Alive

Born in the heart of Kazanchis, Addis Ababa, Fu Fut Coffee was founded on a single belief — that Ethiopia, the birthplace of coffee, deserves a space that honours that legacy with pride and precision.

We source directly from Ethiopia's finest growing regions: the fruity brightness of Guji, the wine-like depth of Yirgacheffe, and the bold earthiness of Sidamo. Every cup we brew is a tribute to the farmers, the land, and the centuries-old tradition of the Ethiopian coffee ceremony.

Beyond coffee, Fu Fut is a gathering place — where Kazanchis residents, visitors, and professionals come to connect, work, and share a meal in a space designed to feel both modern and deeply rooted in culture.

Ethiopia's Coffee Birthplace
"Coffee is not just a drink here — it is a ceremony, a culture, a conversation."

Ethiopia gave the world coffee. At Fu Fut, we honour that gift — from the traditional clay jebena pot to the modern espresso bar. Every sip connects you to a lineage that stretches back centuries across the highlands of this extraordinary country.
