The Founder
HOME  /  ABOUT

Selamawit Bekele

Founder & CEO, Fu Fut Coffee

Heritage poured into every cup — a journey from a grandmother's ceremony to a modern café on Bole Road.

I grew up watching my grandmother roast green beans over charcoal in our Addis Ababa home, filling the air with smoke and story. That ceremony — slow, intentional, communal — is the soul of everything Fu Fut Coffee has become.

In 2017, after a decade working in hospitality abroad, I came home with a simple conviction: that Ethiopian coffee deserved a stage that honored both its heritage and its future. I opened the first Fu Fut Café with three staff, one jebena, and a hand-painted sign.

Today, our team has grown to forty passionate people. We source directly from smallholder farmers in Yirgacheffe, Sidamo, and Guji, roast in small batches, and pour every cup as if welcoming a guest into our own home. The ceremony continues — only the room has grown.

WHAT DRIVES US

Mission, Vision & Promise

#1

Our Mission

To honor the age-old tradition of Ethiopian coffee ceremonies while serving the finest single-origin specialty coffee in a modern, welcoming space.

#2

Our Vision

To see Ethiopian coffee celebrated on the world stage — not as a commodity, but as a cultural treasure, sourced ethically and poured with intention.

#3

Our Promise

Every cup is freshly roasted, hand-poured, and served with the warmth of Ethiopian hospitality. Every farmer is paid fairly. Every guest leaves family.

THE JOURNEY

Milestones Along the Way

2017

The First Café

Opened a tiny 12-seat café in Addis Ababa with one jebena and a borrowed espresso machine.

2019

Bole Road Home

Moved to our flagship location on Bole Road — a space designed to host the full coffee ceremony.

2021

In-House Roastery

Launched our own small-batch roastery to source directly from Yirgacheffe, Sidamo, and Guji farmers.

2023

Ethiopian Coffee Award

Recognized as one of the top three specialty coffee houses in Addis Ababa by the Ethiopian Coffee Association.

2025

Second Location

Expanded to a second café bringing the Fu Fut ceremony to the Kazanchis business district.

2026

Online Ordering

Launched online ordering and delivery so the ceremony can reach homes across the city.
